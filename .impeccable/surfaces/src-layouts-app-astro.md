## Direction contract
THESIS: One next step on the surface, the whole reasoning one tap below. Refuses the instrument-bench dashboard, the career-site card grid, and a lookalike of Duolingo.
OWN-WORLD: "Pafta" — a survey map you draw yourself. Nirengi means triangulation point: every verified work is a point on your map, leagues are elevations (Zemin → Zirve). Signatures: the triangle as the primitive (survey markers, not coins), faint contour lines on hero panels, a dashed survey trail that draws itself, ripple rings instead of bounces, Bricolage Grotesque in sentence case (no uppercase keys), keys with a 3px same-hue lip, a beacon for the streak and a faceted gem for XP, Niri the triangle surveyor who guides, reacts and celebrates. White ground, 2px seams, palette roles unchanged (indigo act, orange seri, cyan doğrulandı, purple lig/topluluk, gold XP, green tamam, red hata).
STORY: A young person sees this week, the next step and the trail from beyan to kurum onayı; an institution is walked through the product by Niri the first time, then sees what waits for its approval and why each candidate ranks.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Bugün — Niri (alive) with a speech bubble, the week strip of triangle markers, the indigo next-step card on a contour panel; below it, "Paftan": the climb map.
FORM: Mechanics inherited from lesson apps (weekly goal, streak, league, quests); geometry, type, iconography and motion are ours. Structure too: the steps climb a contour map bottom-up (no lesson column), quests are waypoints you plant a flag on (no reward chest), proof steps have no lesson chrome (no X + pinned Devam footer). Seed: none — code-led round, direction set by user feedback on 2026-10-08, no concept roll.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
